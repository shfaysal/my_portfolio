I recently built a ride-sharing Driver App designed to help drivers go online, receive trip requests, and complete rides smoothly from start to finish. Drivers can accept/decline requests, navigate with turn-by-turn directions, and manage the full trip flow (arrive → start trip → end trip) with real-time status updates. The app includes earnings tracking, trip history, and wallet/payout summaries, plus built-in safety features like SOS, share trip, and masked calling/chat. On the backend, it supports live driver location, smart matching/dispatch, surge/bonus rules, and reliable notifications—so drivers get fast requests and riders get accurate ETAs.
