## Highlights
- Secure login and registration with JWT and persistent sessions.
- Fast product discovery with category filters and detailed product views.
- Real-time cart updates with local state management.
- Profile editing, settings, and dynamic theme support.
- Full localization for English and Bengali.

## Tech Notes
- 100% Kotlin with Jetpack Compose and Material 3.
- MVVM with clean UI, domain, and data layers.
- Hilt for dependency injection and testable modules.
- Retrofit + OkHttp with Kotlinx Serialization.
- Type-safe Navigation Compose and DataStore for tokens and settings.
